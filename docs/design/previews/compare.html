Ledger puts the answer beside the inputs and sets every figure in JetBrains Mono so the two comparison numbers align digit-for-digit; it is the only direction that ships a dark theme (open ledger.html and press the moon). Broadsheet removes cards entirely — the calculation is set as a worksheet on warm paper, body in Source Serif 4. Contrast. Every colour in all three is derived in OKLCH from the existing brand hues, and every token's ratio is stated in its direction document. Note that #3b82f6 measures 3.68:1 on white and fails AA for body text, which is why light themes use #2c70df or #205ab9 for links — and why the brand blue is most itself in Ledger's dark theme, where it measures 4.85:1.
